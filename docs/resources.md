## Curation Standard

Each proposed resource should include:

1. A direct link and identifiable author or maintainer.
2. A concise description of its relevance to harness engineering.
3. A category and intended audience.
4. Evidence for any performance or reliability claim.
5. Relevant limitations, access requirements, and licensing notes.

Prefer primary sources. Keep descriptions original, identify opinion as opinion, and remove stale or misleading entries during periodic review.
